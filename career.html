Career

The professional story

Nigel Encalada is a cultural heritage expert and applied anthropologist whose work spans the classroom, the national institution, and the international convention. For more than two decades he has worked to safeguard living heritage and to make the case that culture belongs at the center of development — not at its margins.

He led Belize's Institute for Social and Cultural Research for twelve years, coordinated the drafting of the country's first national culture policy, and served as Belize's National Focal Point for the UNESCO 2003 Convention. That national foundation carried him into international practice as a UNESCO Global Facilitator, a consultant to Caribbean governments, and, in 2023, Chairperson of the body that evaluates the world's nominations for intangible cultural heritage. He is now completing a Ph.D. examining how development, heritage, and tourism intersect in Small Island Developing States.
