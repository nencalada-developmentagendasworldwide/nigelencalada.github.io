Development, Living Heritage & Tourism — in conversation

A monthly conversation about what “development” really means — and who gets to define it. Each episode brings together the people shaping the Caribbean region — government officials, tradition-bearers, curators, scholars, entrepreneurs, and community leaders — to examine how living heritage and tourism fit into the development story. How does a minister interpret “development”? A village elder? A tour operator? A young curator? By putting these perspectives in conversation, Development Talk explores where they align, where they collide, and what it all means for the communities whose culture and livelihoods are at stake.

Hosted by Nigel Encalada. New episodes monthly.
